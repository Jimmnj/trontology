# Unified Wave Equation
### A single substrate Lagrangian for gravity, EM, and quantum behavior

---

## 1. Objective
To show that a **single wave equation** for the substrate fields:

- tron density ρ_t  
- ron flow v_r  
- internal order parameter μ  

produces:

- gravity (longitudinal, low‑frequency)  
- electromagnetism (transverse, mid‑frequency)  
- quantum behavior (internal μ‑phase oscillations, high‑frequency)  

as **different modes** of one medium.

---

## 2. Unified Lagrangian
We propose:



\[
\mathcal{L} =
\frac{1}{2} A (\partial_t \rho_t)^2
- \frac{1}{2} B (\nabla \rho_t)^2
+ \frac{1}{2} C (\partial_t v_r)^2
- \frac{1}{2} D (\nabla \times v_r)^2
+ \frac{1}{2} E (\partial_t \mu)^2
- \frac{1}{2} F \mu^2
+ G\,\mu\,\partial_t(\nabla \rho_t)
\]



Interpretation:

- **A, B** → gravity sector  
- **C, D** → EM sector  
- **E, F** → quantum/internal sector  
- **G** → coupling that produces Maxwell curls  

---

## 3. Euler–Lagrange Equations

### 3.1 Gravity Mode (Tron Density)


\[
A\,\partial_t^2 \rho_t - B\,\nabla^2 \rho_t + G\,\partial_t \mu = 0
\]



Long‑wavelength limit → Helmholtz gravity.

---

### 3.2 EM Mode (Ron Flow)


\[
C\,\partial_t^2 v_r - D\,\nabla^2 v_r = 0
\]



Transverse solutions → EM waves.

---

### 3.3 Quantum Mode (Internal μ)


\[
E\,\partial_t^2 \mu + F\,\mu + G\,\partial_t(\nabla \rho_t) = 0
\]



High‑frequency solutions → quantum oscillations.
