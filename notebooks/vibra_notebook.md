## PROVENANCE AND PRIORITY

Richard Marsen conceived the Vibra Theory in the EARLY 1960s --
before the Tron Theory proper (mid-1960s onward). Core concept:
the wave nature of micro-particles is a harmonic oscillation in
the VELOCITY of the particle about its average velocity. The
wavelength of these oscillations is the de Broglie wavelength.

Documented in the 2014 paper (Marsen & Marsen, "The Tron Theory:
A novel concept for the Aether and Matter"), Appendix:

  "the author conceived the concept of the wave nature of
  particles being a harmonic oscillation in the velocity of the
  particle in the early 1960's. He called it the Vibra Theory.
  An almost identical concept was derived independently by the
  late Prof. Petr Beckmann in his book, 'Einstein Plus Two' [...]
  He deserves credit for the concept; he derives it directly from
  E/M theory with the assumption of a local entrained aether."

Priority note: Richard's conception (early 1960s) PRECEDES
Beckmann's publication (1987). Richard's version was descriptive/
mechanistic; Beckmann's is derived from EM theory with entrained
ether. The 2014 paper generously credits Beckmann for the
derivation. Both statements are true simultaneously: Richard has
conceptual priority; Beckmann has derivational priority.

## WHAT VIBRA SAYS (from the 2014 paper, Section 4)

1. Fundamental particles (electrons, nucleons) are compressed tron
   cores surrounded by a less dense "parfield" of tron layers.

2. The parfield radius inherently oscillates harmonically in its
   ambient tronos. This causes the particle's instantaneous
   velocity (and kinetic energy) to vary harmonically about its
   average observed velocity.

3. The wavelength of these velocity oscillations IS the de Broglie
   wavelength. Wave-particle duality is dispelled: the "wave" is a
   real kinematic modulation of a real particle, not an ontological
   dual nature.

4. Position and momentum are simultaneously definite at all times.
   Indeterminacy is epistemic, not ontological. QM phenomena arise
   from deterministic, causal interaction of the particle with the
   tronos.

5. The particle oscillations generate EM waves in the tronos at the
   particle frequency, f = E/h. Radiant energy is emitted as
   truncated continuous wave trains (pulses) -- e.g. ~10 million
   waves long from electron orbital shifts. These pulses are what
   orthodox physics calls photons.

## WHERE VIBRA MEETS THE QUANTUM WALL
## [carried over from July 11 GPRF-project session on "EM is only waves"]

The 2014 paper's photon-as-pulse picture (truncated wave trains)
faces the same beamsplitter constraints as any wave-only EM:

- ANTIBUNCHING (Kimble-Dagenais-Mandel 1977; Grangier-Roger-Aspect
  1986): single-emitter light at a beamsplitter gives g2(0) ~ 0 --
  one detector clicks, never both. A classical pulse divides its
  energy at a beamsplitter; both detectors should sometimes fire.
  A pulse-train photon, as stated, does not survive this unless
  the DETECTION process localizes the full quantum of energy at
  one core nonlocally.

- The trontology hierarchy has the resource for this: the RON
  near-field channel (v_ron >> c, June 27 2026 entry, BH2 track).
  Bell nonlocality requires superluminal coordination in a
  preferred frame; the ron channel provides exactly that, for
  independent reasons (Van Flandern, Richard's oscillator origin).
  OPEN PROBLEM: articulate mechanistically how ron-channel
  coordination collapses a spatially divided wave train's energy
  into one absorption event. This is Vibra's hardest open problem
  and the price of keeping determinism.

- Double slit with single particles: Vibra's velocity-modulation
  picture must explain how one particle's modulation produces
  interference statistics across an ensemble. Beckmann's EM
  derivation may address this -- check Einstein Plus Two.
