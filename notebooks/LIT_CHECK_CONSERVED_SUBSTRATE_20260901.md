## 3. HALO EDGES ARE ALREADY OBSERVED — THE SPLASHBACK RADIUS

"Detection of the splashback radius and halo assembly bias of massive
galaxy clusters"; also AMICO/KiDS-1000, A&A (2026); "The Edge of the
Galaxy" (2020); "The Outermost Edges of the Milky Way Halo from Galaxy
Kinematics", ApJL (2021).
So "the halo has an edge and lensing can see it" is established, and the
Tron claim is NOT the first halo-edge proposal.
BUT THE TWO DIFFER, AND MEASURABLY:
  - MECHANISM. Splashback is the apocentre of material on first infall.

    formation. Inadmissible here, and it is a Tier-2 term under the
    canon terminology law. The Tron edge is a mass-conservation
    boundary and needs no accretion history.
  - SCALE. Splashback sits at ~1-1.5 x R_200m; for an L* galaxy that is
    ~300-450 kpc. The territory estimate is 2-3 Mpc — a factor ~7
    larger. THE PREDICTIONS DO NOT COINCIDE.
That factor of ~7 is the discriminator, and it is in the radial range
the Mistele stack already covers.
