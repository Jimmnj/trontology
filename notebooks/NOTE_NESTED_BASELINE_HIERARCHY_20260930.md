## 3a. THE CMB DIPOLE (JM 9/30 question) [T4]
NOT the dipole itself: it is kinematic (370 km/s toward Leo), confirmed independently by Planck's
~5 sigma detection of aberration + modulation of small-scale anisotropies. Intrinsic component
bounded to a small fraction. A tilted Phi_bg cannot be the main dipole.
THE ANOMALY IS THE ROOM: Ellis-Baldwin test. Kinematic dipole predicts a source-count dipole of
fixed amplitude/direction. Secrest+2021 (CatWISE quasars), Secrest+2022 (quasars + NVSS radio):
matter dipole ~2x the kinematic prediction, ~5 sigma, same direction. Unresolved.
LOPSIDED-LEVEL PREDICTION: a gradient in the next baseline tilts Phi_bg -> lopsided rho_nb at
our level (same mechanism as a galaxy halo under external field, gprf LIT sec 4c item 3) -> an
INTRINSIC matter dipole independent of our velocity. Matter dipole = kinematic + intrinsic;
temperature dipole stays kinematic. Exactly the anomaly's shape. Falsifiable: if the excess
resolves into systematics, the tilt is bounded to ~0.
OBJECTION (must be answered before any claim): in GR a super-horizon potential gradient gives NO
dipole at first order -- Sachs-Wolfe and Doppler cancel (Grishchuk & Zel'dovich 1978; Erickcek,
Carroll & Kamionkowski 2008 "tilted universe"); leading survivor is a QUADRUPOLE. In a static
absolute-time reading the cancellation is not guaranteed, but that has to be SHOWN. Tron-side
calculation, not an assumption.
BH2 CROSS-REFERENCE: the CMB dipole velocity is THE preferred-frame velocity; BH2's premise is a
preferred frame. The intrinsic-dipole reading says the CMB frame and the matter frame differ
slightly. Real link between the two programs. FIREWALL: Tron/BH2 only, never GPRF.
