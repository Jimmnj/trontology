## 1. Core Ontology
The substrate consists of three interacting fields:

- **Tron density ρ_t(x,t)**  
  Defines local configuration; gradients correspond to E.

- **Ron flow v_r(x,t)**  
  Defines dynamic response; circulation corresponds to B.

- **Internal order parameter μ(x,t)**  
  Encodes substrate reorientation, overshoot, and induced anisotropy.

These are not particles; they are coarse‑grained descriptors of a continuous medium.

## 2. Emergent Electromagnetism
### 2.1 Field Identification
- \(E = \nabla \rho_t\)  
- \(B = \text{curl}(v_r)\)

### 2.2 Role of μ(x,t)
μ responds to ∂E/∂t:



\[
\tau^2 \ddot{\mu} + \tau \dot{\mu} + \mu = \beta\,\frac{\partial E}{\partial t}
\]



μ is 90° out of phase with E, but B is not.  
Thus:

- μ carries the lag  
- E and B remain in phase  
- Maxwell’s equations emerge in the coarse‑grained limit

### 2.3 Transverse Waves Without a Solid Ether
The substrate has:

- no shear modulus  
- no permanent rigidity  
- no preferred frame  

Transverse EM waves arise from **induced stiffness** via μ, not from a solid medium.
