## 3. JM'S ARGUMENT: NO DE IS A MAJOR BLOW TO LCDM (T3, to assess)
LCDM = "Lambda-CDM": the two letters ARE dark energy (Lambda) and cold
dark matter. Remove Lambda and it is not a modified LCDM, it is a
different model. Specifics of what breaks:
  (a) Flatness + the budget. CMB says the universe is spatially flat,
      Omega_total = 1. Baryons ~5%, CDM ~25%, so Lambda ~70% is what
      CLOSES the budget. Without DE and without adding ~70% more
      matter, flat-universe LCDM loses its energy budget. (A static /
      non-expanding reading, JM's, sidesteps this by dropping the FLRW
      closure condition entirely — but that is a wholesale change, not
      a tweak.)
  (b) Age. Without Lambda, a matter-only expanding FLRW universe is
      YOUNGER than its oldest stars (the pre-1998 age problem). DE
      was part of what resolved it. A static/tired-light cosmology
      removes the age problem differently (no expansion age at all).
  (c) Structure-growth timing and the late-time ISW cross-correlation
      are DE-dependent in LCDM.
  CAUTION (state honestly): "no DE blows up LCDM" is true for LCDM AS A
  MODEL, but the mainstream fallback is not "no cosmology" — it is
  evolving-DE, or curvature, or a local-inhomogeneity model that keeps
  expansion. So Sarkar/DESI removing Lambda strengthens the case that
  the STANDARD model is wrong; it does NOT by itself select a static or
  substrate cosmology. That inference is a separate, heavier argument
  (H0-as-length, Sciama static budget) that must be made on its own
  merits. Do not let "no DE" smuggle in "therefore Tron cosmology."
