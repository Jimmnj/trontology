# Unified Wave Equation Sketch
### *Toward a single substrate Lagrangian for gravity, EM, and quantum behavior*

---

## 1. Goal
Construct a single wave equation whose different modes correspond to:

- gravity (longitudinal, low‑frequency)  
- electromagnetism (transverse, mid‑frequency)  
- quantum behavior (internal μ‑phase oscillations, high‑frequency)  

---

## 2. Substrate Variables
We treat the substrate as a continuum with:

- density field ρ_t(x,t)  
- flow field v_r(x,t)  
- internal orientation μ(x,t)  

We seek a Lagrangian \( \mathcal{L}(\rho_t, v_r, \mu) \).

---

## 3. Prototype Lagrangian
A minimal unified Lagrangian:



\[
\mathcal{L} =
\frac{1}{2} A (\partial_t \rho_t)^2
- \frac{1}{2} B (\nabla \rho_t)^2
+ \frac{1}{2} C (\partial_t v_r)^2
- \frac{1}{2} D (\nabla \times v_r)^2
+ \frac{1}{2} E (\partial_t \mu)^2
- \frac{1}{2} F \mu^2
+ G\,\mu\,\partial_t(\nabla \rho_t)
\]



Interpretation:

- first line → gravity mode  
- second line → EM mode  
- third line → quantum/internal mode  
- last term → coupling that produces Maxwell’s curls  

---

## 4. Euler–Lagrange Equations
Variation yields three coupled wave equations:

### 4.1 Tron Density (Gravity Mode)


\[
A\,\partial_t^2 \rho_t - B\,\nabla^2 \rho_t + G\,\partial_t \mu = 0
\]



Long‑wavelength limit → Helmholtz gravity.

---

### 4.2 Ron Flow (EM Mode)


\[
C\,\partial_t^2 v_r - D\,\nabla^2 v_r = 0
\]



Transverse solutions → EM waves.

---

### 4.3 Internal Response μ (Quantum Mode)


\[
E\,\partial_t^2 \mu + F\,\mu + G\,\partial_t(\nabla \rho_t) = 0
\]



High‑frequency solutions → quantum oscillations.

---

## 5. Mode Structure
### Gravity
- longitudinal  
- low‑frequency  
- sourced by baryonic mass  

### EM
- transverse  
- mid‑frequency  
- sourced by ∂E/∂t via μ  

### Quantum
- internal  
- high‑frequency  
- phase‑coherent  
- potentially gives rise to ħ  

---

## 6. Unification Summary
All three sectors arise from:

- one substrate  
- one Lagrangian  
- one wave equation  
- three mode families  

This is a unification without strings, loops, or quantizing spacetime.
